Although the raw CCA shows a relatively high loss on ignition (LOI=11.29%), this is expected due to residual organics and moisture. LOI values are known to decrease significantly upon incineration at elevated temperatures, which enhances the ash’s reactivity and stability. Therefore, CCA in this study was considered as a supplementary material only after calcination, particularly in blended systems with RHA, where it may contribute to long-term matrix refinement and promote sustainable material utilization. Low CaO levels in both ashes (1.05% in RHA, 2.45% in CCA) confirm their non- cementitious nature, reinforcing their role as supplementary cementitious materials. These compositional characteristics form the basis for selecting and interpreting subsequent tests, including SAI, ASR, capillary absorption, microstructural analysis, and mechanical strengths.
