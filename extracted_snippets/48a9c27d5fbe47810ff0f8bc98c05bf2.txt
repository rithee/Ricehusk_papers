Results and discussion The investigation of the test results and the discussion are presented in the following sections: Specific gravity and compaction properties of composite mixes The specific gravity (GS) values of different composite mix proportions are presented in Figs. 2a to e. CS exhibited a specific gravity of 3.50, which falls within the reported range of 3.37–3.97 [57], while RHA showed a lower value of 2.06.
