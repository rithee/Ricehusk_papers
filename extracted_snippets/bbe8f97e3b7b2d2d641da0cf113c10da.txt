The producer gas contains combustible components such as carbon monoxide (CO), hydrogen (H2), and methane (CH4). This gas can be used as a fuel in boilers, engines or gas turbines. The advantage of gasiﬁca- tion is that the producer gas can be used in prime movers (gas engines, gas turbines, fuel cells) to generate electricity at higher efﬁciency (Dinkelbach, 2000).
