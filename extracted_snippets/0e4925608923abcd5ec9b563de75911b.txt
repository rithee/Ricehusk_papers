Conclusion In this research, a comprehensive analysis of 633 data sets on the compressive strength (CS) of fly ash-based concrete was conducted using information gathered from past journal papers (Song et al., 2021), (Song et al., 2022), (Mahajan and Bhagat, 2022). The primary objective of this study was to compare the performance of non-ensemble and ensemble-selected machine learning (ML) models in predicting the CS of concrete containing fly ash. The non-ensemble ML algorithm like Mul­ tiple LR and SVR and ensemble ML algorithms like AdaBoost regressor, Random Forest regression, XGBoost regressor, and bagging regressor were employed. The results obtained from both types of models were compared to assess their predictive capabilities. The following key findings emerged from this study. 1. The substantial evidence in favor of using AI approaches to predict the compressive strength of concrete containing fly ash comes from the good agreement between the predicted and experimental results. All the models other than multiple LR can be considered highly reliable predictive models, i.e., models with R2 values more than 0.85. 2. The ensemble ML models demonstrated superior performance, as evidenced by higher values of R2 and lower errors (MSE and MAE), or reduced variance between the actual and predicted outcomes. 3.
