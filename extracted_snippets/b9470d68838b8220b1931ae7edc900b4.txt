Discussion In Europe, America, Japan, and other developed countries, the construction of high-rise buildings and infrastructure has undergone large-scale rapid development, which has now transitioned to the stage of maintenance and repair of existing structures. The repair and renovation of existing building structures have emerged as a prominent research area in current engineering construction. The sub­ stantial economic burden brought on by the deterioration of concrete structures has far exceeded initial expectations, prompting widespread attention to the durability of concrete structures as a primary indicator of structural quality. NDT techniques are widely employed to evaluate the quality of concrete structures, particularly in terms of concrete compressive strength. Existing studies have revealed that concrete mix design and curing age are often overlooked in predicting concrete compressive strength using NDT and machine learning methods, even though they significantly impact the strength prediction. Therefore, this study aims to introduce an effective approach for predicting concrete compressive strength by integrating concrete mix design and NDT test results. The study establishes a SVR model for concrete compressive strength and a BPNN prediction model based on NDT test outcomes, considering the influence of concrete mix design and curing age.
