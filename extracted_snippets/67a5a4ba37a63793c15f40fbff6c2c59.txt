Regression coefficients are referred to by the abbreviation "Coef," and these coefficients provide information on the strength and direction of the link between the input and output variables. Additionally, the constant terms that are multiplied in a regression equation are these coefficients. While the sign indicates the direction of the relationship, the size of the coefficients tells us how much of an impact a term has on the response variables. The standard error of the coefficients is denoted by the phrase "SE Coef." It demonstrates the accuracy with which can measure the coefficients; the less the error, the greater accuracy with which we can estimate the coefficients. Based on a 95% confidence interval, the "p" values demonstrate the relevance of the term in the regression equation.
