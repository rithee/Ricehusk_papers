Sample Analysis The specimen's elemental composition was characterized through the analytical technique of energy-dispersive X-ray (EDX) spectroscopy, whereby a sample was bombarded by a targeted electron beam which displaced electrons from their atomic orbits, while distinctive X-rays are emitted. The bombardment of the sample with electrons causes ejection of electrons, with vacancies being created and populated by electrons of a lower energy, while as they relax excess energy in the X-ray form is subsequently released. During the period of relaxation, the energy given is absolutely distinct for each and every of the element within present withing the sample. In essence, each and every element possesses their own completely unique X-ray signature, as such, the X-rays emitted from the specimen furnished qualitative compositional insights [14, 24].
