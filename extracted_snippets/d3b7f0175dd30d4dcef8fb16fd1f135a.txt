The mixing process was carried out using a concrete mixer to ensure uniform blending of cement, rice husk ash, aggregates, and water. Dry components were ﬁrst mixed, followed by gradual addition of water for proper hydration. Stirring was performed using a vibration table, where the concrete was poured into moulds and compacted through vibration to eliminate air pockets and enhance density. This combination ensured a homogeneous mix, reduced porosity, and improved the structural integrity of the concrete products. The average mixing time was kept between 6–8 min. This is because, increased mixing time ensures uniform dispersion of ﬁne RHA and WMS particles, preventing segregation due to their low densities. It also facilitates better integration with cement and aggregates, improving the homogeneity of the mix. This results in consistent workability and enhanced performance of the concrete.
