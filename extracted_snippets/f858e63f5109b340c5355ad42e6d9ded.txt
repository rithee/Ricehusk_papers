Hydration properties of cement blended with RHA The hydration process of blends of RHA and cement, compared with conventional pozzolanic materials, is more complicated due to the adsorption and release of water by the porous structure of RHA. The effects of RHA on cement hydration include dilution effect, chemical effect and internal curing effect [38]. The dilution effect represents the decrease of cement content. The chemical effect is the pozzolanic reaction of SiO2 with CH [39,40], and the internal curing effect shows the promotion of cement hydration by the continuous release of water from RHA. That is, when the rel- ative humidity in the matrix decreases, water is released from the pores of RHA particles and promotes the further hydration of the surrounding cement particles [41]. 3.1. Hydration process of cement blended with RHA The effect of RHA on the cement hydration is closely related to the water-binder ratio and the content of RHA [15]. With the increase of RHA content, the amount of cement in the pastes decreases and the main hydration exothermic peak is delayed with ﬁxed w/b ratio. When the RHA content is constant, with higher w/b the main hydration exothermic peak and acceleration period appear slightly earlier.
