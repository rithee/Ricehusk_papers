Along with developments in construction material technology, studies on the prediction and optimization of compressive strength with various alternative materials, such as RCA, SCC, bio-composite aggregate, nano silica, magnesium silicate, magnesium phosphate, and plastic aggregate, have begun in multiple regions. In the future, the study of these algorithms still has excellent potential. XGBoost A limitation of GBoost is its prolonged iteration process and frequent occurrence of overfitting [60]. The XGBoost method was developed to improve the limitations of the GBoost algorithm and mitigate overfitting [77]. As a more advanced iteration of GBoost, the XGBoost algorithm is frequently used for model-classifying regression [78]. This algorithm consists of several decision trees, which are trained using the prediction outcomes of the previous trees. The final result of the model prediction is the summation of the prediction outcomes for each tree, as shown in Equation 1 [78].
